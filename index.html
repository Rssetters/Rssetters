Questions clients ask us

Something else on your mind? Ask us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.

Why do you charge a commission?

The fee covers the real costs of setting up and running your campaigns. The commission means we only earn more when you sign more clients, so we're focused on the same thing you are: paying clients, not just booked calls.

Which plan should I choose?

If you're new to cold email, Pilot lets you test it with little risk. Growth gives campaigns enough time to improve and is where most coaches see steady results. Partner suits coaches who already sign clients from outreach and want the lowest commission as they grow.

Will cold email hurt my main domain?

No. We send from separate domains set up only for outreach, so your main domain and everyday email stay safe.

How soon will I see results?

New inboxes need about two weeks to warm up before sending, so the first stage is setup. Once campaigns are live, we improve them week by week. We'll give you a realistic timeline on the discovery call.

Do you work with my industry?

We work with both B2B and B2C businesses, and much of our experience is with coaches. Tell us about your offer and we'll be honest about whether cold email is a good fit.

Isn't cold email just spam?

Not when it's done properly. We send relevant, personal emails to carefully chosen people at sensible volumes, and every prospect can easily opt out.
